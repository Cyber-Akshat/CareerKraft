# CareerKraft Project Plan

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** CareerKraft

**Project Type:** Career discovery, skills development and opportunity platform

**Target Users:** Primarily students and young people aged approximately 15–25 who are uncertain about their future career direction.

**Project Purpose:**  
CareerKraft is designed to help young people move from career uncertainty to practical progress.

Rather than simply recommending a career through a quiz, CareerKraft aims to guide users through a full development journey:

**Discover → Choose → Learn → Build → Prove → Develop Skills → Gain Experience → Apply**

The platform combines personalised career matching, structured roadmaps, learning modules, practical activities, evidence submission, AI-powered feedback, verified skills and future access to relevant work experience and apprenticeship opportunities.

---

# 2. Project Problem

Many students are expected to make important career decisions before they have had enough opportunity to understand different careers or experience what the work is actually like.

Traditional career quizzes may suggest possible jobs, but they often stop after providing recommendations.

This creates several problems:

- students still do not know what to do next
- career recommendations can feel generic
- students have little opportunity to test whether they genuinely enjoy a career
- students may struggle to build relevant skills
- students often claim skills without having evidence to demonstrate them
- finding relevant work experience can be difficult
- career guidance, learning, skill building and opportunities are often spread across different platforms

CareerKraft aims to connect these stages into one continuous experience.

---

# 3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of CareerKraft are to:

1. Help users identify career paths that suit their interests, strengths, subjects and working preferences.

2. Give users clear next steps rather than stopping at a career recommendation.

3. Provide personalised Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced career roadmaps.

4. Give users practical activities that allow them to experience aspects of a career.

5. Provide learning modules and useful educational resources.

6. Allow users to upload real evidence of their work.

7. Use Lumia to review supported evidence and provide constructive feedback.

8. Build an evidence-backed skills profile.

9. Help users understand their progress over time.

10. Eventually connect users with relevant work experience, apprenticeships and employer opportunities.

11. Support schools and organisations that want to provide structured career development.

# 5. Core Features

## Career Assessment

Users complete a personalised assessment covering areas such as:

- interests
- favourite subjects
- strengths
- soft skills
- preferred working style

Lumia analyses the responses and recommends suitable careers.

---

## Career Matching

CareerKraft provides several personalised career matches rather than forcing the user into one option.

Career matches can include:

- career title
- match indicator
- explanation of why the career may suit the user
- ability to explore and select a career

---

- technology

- engineering
- business
- finance
- healthcare
- psychology
- law
- education
- marketing
- media
- design
- science

## Personalised Career Roadmaps

After selecting a career, the user receives a structured roadmap.

Each roadmap contains three levels:

**Beginner**  
Introduces the career and builds foundational knowledge.

**Intermediate**  
Introduces more realistic challenges and applied work.

**Advanced**  
Provides more complex projects and professional-style activities.

---

## Learning Modules

Each roadmap activity can contain:

- introduction
- learning objectives
- key concepts
- examples
- practical task
- recommended resources
- evidence instructions
- verification criteria
- skills that may be demonstrated
- reflection questions

---

## Practical Career Activities

Activities may include:

- research
- projects
- challenges
- simulations
- written tasks
- technical exercises

- analysis

- PDFs

Lumia evaluates submitted evidence against the requirements of the activity.

Possible outcomes include:

- what should be improved

- data analysis

The aim is to build skills based on demonstrated work rather than self-declaration alone.

# 14. Risks

## AI Reliability

AI may provide incorrect or weak recommendations.

**Mitigation:**  
Use structured prompts, validation, trusted sources, human review and clear limitations.

## Evidence Verification

AI may incorrectly approve or reject work.

**Mitigation:**  
Use activity-specific verification criteria, confidence thresholds and human-review fallback.

## Privacy

CareerKraft may handle data belonging to young users.

**Mitigation:**  
Apply strict data minimisation, secure storage, privacy controls and appropriate safeguarding policies.

## File Security

Users may upload unsafe files.

**Mitigation:**  
Block executable formats, never execute uploaded code, restrict file types and introduce malware scanning before large-scale deployment.

## Career Accuracy

Career requirements can change.

**Mitigation:**  
Use reliable and current sources where qualifications, professional requirements or eligibility are discussed.

## User Retention

Students may complete the initial quiz and not return.

**Mitigation:**  
Make CareerKraft's value continue after the quiz through roadmaps, modules, skills, evidence and opportunities.

## Competition

CareerKraft competes indirectly with career platforms, educational tools and AI assistants.

**Mitigation:**  
Differentiate through the complete journey from discovery to evidence-backed skills and opportunities.

# 16. Long-Term Vision

CareerKraft aims to become a platform where a student can begin with:

**“I have no idea what I want to do.”**

and gradually reach:

**“I know the direction I want to explore, I understand the career, I have completed relevant work, I have evidence of my skills, and I know what opportunity to pursue next.”**

CareerKraft should eventually connect four areas that are normally separated:

**Career Guidance + Learning + Evidence + Opportunities**

The long-term goal is not simply to tell students what career they should choose.

It is to help them **discover it, experience it and build toward it.**

**CareerKraft — Your future should feel like a path, not a guess.**
