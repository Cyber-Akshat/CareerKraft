A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Curiosity
welcome.

You don’t need to have all the answers before you start.

What if I don’t know which career I want?+

That’s a good place to start. Share your interests and strengths, then explore three career matches and why they could suit you before choosing your direction.

What does “skills backed by evidence” mean?+

You submit work for an activity, and it’s reviewed against the activity’s requirements. Skills demonstrated in approved work are linked to that evidence, so you can see what’s behind your progress.

How does Lumia help me?+

Lumia is your AI career companion. It uses your roadmap, current activity and saved progress to help you understand the task, decide what to focus on and reflect on the skills you’re building.

Can I get started for free?+

Yes. CareerKraft is free during early access. Create an account and begin by exploring careers that match your interests and strengths.
