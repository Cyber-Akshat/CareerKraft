Privacy in simple terms

CareerKraft uses information you give us to provide your account, career recommendations, personalised roadmap, activities, Skills Profile and Lumia.

Your evidence is private by default. We do not sell your personal information. Some information is processed by service providers that help us operate CareerKraft, including AI services.

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information. Account settings also lets you download your account data or delete your account.

Draft for operator review

This notice describes the current Beta. The operator’s postal contact address, public hosting and email providers, and applicable international transfer arrangements still need confirming before public release. The contact name and email below match the currently published Terms of Use.

1. Who runs CareerKraft

The operator currently identified in our Terms of Use is Esha Prasad, trading as CareerKraft. The operator is the data controller: the person responsible for deciding why and how CareerKraft uses personal information.

For privacy requests and support, email careerkraft.official@gmail.com. You can use the subject “Privacy request”.

You do not need a CareerKraft account to contact us about privacy. If the operator changes, we will update this notice.

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy covers visitors, people joining the early access list, account holders and people contacting CareerKraft. CareerKraft is intended for users aged 15 and over, including users aged 15–17. We do not ask for a date of birth or claim to have verified everyone’s age.

Following a link to a learning resource, Google or another external service takes you to a service with its own privacy information. A school does not automatically receive access to your account, evidence or Lumia conversations.

3. Information we collect

Most information comes from you, your use of CareerKraft, or results generated from that information:

  • Account and profile: your name, email address, account creation and verification details, a hashed password if you use one, and an optional profile photo or chosen character. We also save appearance and reduced-motion preferences.
  • Google sign-in, if you choose it: a Google account identifier, email address, email-verification information and your name. Google supplies this information when you sign in or connect your account. We do not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail messages, Drive files or contacts.
  • Career discovery: quiz answers about interests, subjects, strengths and preferred ways of working; recommended careers, match scores and explanations; and your selected career.
  • Your learning journey: roadmaps, generated learning content, activities started or completed, progress, levels and related timestamps.
  • Evidence and skills: written responses, uploaded files, filenames, file types and sizes, extracted content and processing details, review outcomes and feedback, and skills identified from your work.
  • Lumia: your messages, AI replies, conversation records and timestamps, and relevant CareerKraft context used to answer your questions.
  • Security and technical information: session and verification records, sign-in activity times, temporary Google sign-in records, request and error information, and IP-based and account-based rate-limit identifiers. The app hashes identifiers used in its persistent authentication rate-limit store. Servers and service providers also receive connection information needed to deliver requests.
  • Updates and enquiries: your email address and signup time if you join early access, and the information you send when asking for help or exercising your rights.

Some account details are necessary to create and secure an account. Quiz answers are needed to personalise recommendations, and evidence is needed to review an activity that requires it. A profile photo, Google sign-in and early access signup are optional.

4. Evidence uploads

Your evidence is private by default: CareerKraft does not publish it in a public gallery or make it available to other students. You and authorised CareerKraft reviewers can access evidence where needed to review an activity, resolve a problem or protect the service. Reviewers may also see your name, email and related activity details.

Depending on the file, CareerKraft may:

  • extract text from documents, PDFs, presentations, spreadsheets, notebooks or code;
  • send images to an AI service to describe visible content;
  • send audio, including audio extracted from a video, for transcription; and
  • sample up to three video frames for AI image analysis.

Extracted text, transcripts, image descriptions, selected frames, supporting notes and activity criteria may be processed by Groq to review your work and identify skills. The original upload and the review information are kept with your account. Processing can be incomplete or fail; a result does not mean that every page, cell or video frame was examined.

Uploaded code is read as content and is not executed. CareerKraft does not run uploaded programs, render uploaded websites, execute notebook cells or calculate uploaded spreadsheet formulas.

Remove unnecessary personal information before uploading. Do not include passwords, API keys, identity documents, private contact details, medical records or another person’s work or personal information without an appropriate reason and permission.

5. Lumia and AI processing

CareerKraft uses Groq for AI features, including career recommendations, roadmaps, learning content, evidence review and Lumia replies. These features may use relevant quiz answers, career choices, roadmap and activity progress, skills and feedback.

When you message Lumia, relevant recent messages and CareerKraft context, including the activity or page you are viewing, may be sent to Groq. Evidence review may send the content described in section 4. Do not put information in a conversation or upload that you would not want processed for these purposes.

The companion context is designed to exclude account passwords, session secrets and private storage paths. Information you type or put in a file may still contain personal details; CareerKraft cannot promise to detect and remove all of them.

AI produces career suggestions and review feedback by comparing your answers or work with career information and activity criteria. Results can be wrong or incomplete. They can affect recommendations, recorded skills or activity progress inside CareerKraft; they are not decisions about school admissions, employment, qualifications or access to essential services. Contact us if you want to question a result or ask for a review.

CareerKraft does not use your evidence or conversations to train its own AI models. Groq’s processing and retention are subject to its terms and data controls; we do not promise that every provider request has zero retention. See section 10.

  • Remember your progress, review evidence, give feedback and build your Skills Profile.
  • Provide personalised Lumia conversations and remember their history.
  • Apply your profile and accessibility preferences, provide downloads and carry out deletion requests.
  • Protect accounts, prevent abuse, investigate errors and fix technical problems.
  • Respond to enquiries and privacy requests, and send early access updates you requested.

We do not sell personal information or use the current Beta to serve targeted advertising.

7. Our lawful bases

Data protection law requires a reason, called a lawful basis, for using personal information. Accepting our Terms is not consent to every use of your information. The proposed bases for this Beta are set out below for operator confirmation.

Accounts, career discovery, roadmaps, evidence, skills and Lumia
Legitimate interests — UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Our interest is providing the private career-learning service you choose to use, remembering your work and giving relevant guidance and feedback. This includes necessary processing by the providers described below.
Security, sign-in, verification, password resets and technical support
Legitimate interests — Article 6(1)(f). Our interests are protecting accounts and private work, preventing misuse and keeping the service reliable. Necessary security checks are part of using an account.
Optional early access emails
Consent — Article 6(1)(a). Joining the updates list is a separate choice. Email the privacy contact to leave the list or withdraw this consent. This does not affect your account or earlier lawful processing.
Meeting applicable legal duties
Legal obligation — Article 6(1)(c). Where a duty applies, this includes handling data protection requests and responding to a legally binding requirement. We will explain the relevant duty where appropriate.

Legitimate interests must be balanced against your rights and reasonable expectations, with particular care for anyone under 18. They do not permit us to use information simply because it would be useful. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. We do not ask you to provide sensitive information such as health or religious information for the service.

10. How long information is kept

Keeping information and allowing a security link to work are different things. An expired link stops working, but its database record is not necessarily erased at that moment.

Account, learning, evidence, skills and Lumia records
Kept while the account exists unless replaced or removed through an available account action or a request we carry out. Starting a new Lumia conversation does not delete older conversations. The Beta has no automatic inactive-account deletion schedule.
Uploaded files and profile photos
Kept privately with the account. Replacing eligible evidence or a profile photo triggers removal of the replaced file; account deletion triggers removal of linked files. Temporary media working files are removed after processing on a best-effort basis. Failed cleanup may need manual attention.
Sessions and temporary sign-in records
By default a session has a 24-hour maximum and a 30-minute inactivity limit; the operator can configure these limits. Verification, password-reset and email-change links are valid for one hour. A Google sign-in flow lasts up to ten minutes. Records are removed when used, replaced, revoked or cleaned up by relevant account/sign-in actions; there is no general timed purge of all expired records.
Security counters and technical records
Authentication rate-limit records use time windows and expired counters are cleaned up during subsequent authentication requests. Other server, email or provider logs have their own storage and deletion arrangements. We do not claim a fixed automatic deletion period for these records.
Early access and support
Early access signup information remains until removed following a withdrawal/deletion request or the matching account-deletion action. Enquiries, sent emails and development email previews are separate records and may need manual removal. The app does not automatically purge them after a stated number of days.
AI provider records
Groq says ordinary inference inputs and outputs are not retained by default, but reliability or abuse investigations can involve retention for up to 30 days, or longer if legally required. Usage metadata is retained separately. This is the provider’s policy, not a CareerKraft automatic deletion schedule or a claim that our account has zero-retention settings enabled. See Groq’s current data information.

Separate provider records, emails or any independently held backups are not automatically erased by deleting a live account. The deletion action also does not securely wipe every underlying database or journal copy. A privacy request can ask us to review those records too. We do not promise instant removal from every historic copy or from a provider’s systems.

11. Cookies and browser storage

The current Beta does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The cookie banner lets you accept or reject optional cookies; essential sign-in and security cookies remain active whichever you choose.

  • __Host-ck_session in production, or ck_session locally: a necessary authentication cookie that keeps you signed in. Its default maximum lifetime is 24 hours. The server also ends sessions after the configured inactivity limit, which is 30 minutes by default. It is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and production cookies use Secure.
  • ck_oauth: a necessary security cookie used when you choose Google sign-in, account connection or identity confirmation. It lasts up to ten minutes and is cleared on the callback. It is HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax and Secure in production.
  • Local browser storage: remembers your theme, reduced-motion preference, accessibility options in careerkraft-accessibility, Lumia’s last-read message marker and which roadmap-level notifications you have already seen. Accessibility options include your font, text size, contrast, colour saturation and cursor size. Saving preferences also stores these choices with your account. These values can remain until you clear site data. Temporary session storage passes level-completion information between pages and is removed when read or when the browser session ends.

Your choice is saved in local browser storage as careerkraft-cookie-consent. We ask again after 180 days. You can change your choice at any time in Account settings under Cookies or using Cookie settings in the footer of any page. Changes save immediately and apply to the current browser. Accepting optional cookies does not currently enable any analytics or advertising cookies.

When you finish or skip the dashboard tour, we remember that choice in your account and in an account-specific careerkraft-dashboard-tour: browser record so the introduction does not repeat. You can replay it using Dashboard tour on the dashboard.

You can remove cookies and site storage in your browser. Removing the session cookie signs that browser out; blocking necessary cookies prevents account features from working. Browser preference records are separate from the account database and may remain on a shared device after sign-out or account deletion.

12. Children’s privacy

Some users are under 18, so privacy explanations should be easy to understand and information should be limited to what the feature needs. We provide short notices beside the quiz, evidence uploader and Lumia as well as this policy.

Evidence and conversations are private from other students and the public. We do not require a profile photo, school name, home address or date of birth for a standard account. Do not upload unnecessary details about yourself, classmates, family members or teachers.

You can ask for help with a privacy request, including help from a trusted adult. Being a parent, teacher or school does not by itself give someone unrestricted access to your account. Contact us if someone under 15 has registered or if you think information about a child needs urgent attention.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access your personal information, correct it, have it deleted, restrict its use or receive a portable copy where that right applies.

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Tell us what processing you object to and why. We will consider the request and explain any grounds for continuing. You can object to direct marketing at any time.

Where we rely on consent, such as requested early access updates, you can withdraw it by emailing us. Withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of processing that happened before it.

Use Account settings to edit supported account details, download your account data or delete the account. For other requests, email careerkraft.official@gmail.com. Explain what you need; do not send identity documents unless we explain why a proportionate identity check is necessary.

Rights have legal conditions and exceptions. We will explain any refusal or extension and your complaint options. Children have privacy rights too.

14. Account and data deletion

To delete your account, sign in and open Account settings → Your data, your choice → Delete account. Type DELETE and confirm with your current password, or recent Google identity confirmation if you have a Google-only account.

The account-deletion action removes live database records for your profile, career assessments and matches, selected career, roadmaps, progress, evidence, Skills Profile and Lumia conversations. It revokes sessions and account tokens, removes a matching early access signup, and attempts to remove linked evidence and profile-photo files. Deleted accounts cannot be restored through the app.

You can download your account data before deleting. The JSON download contains saved account and journey information, including evidence details and conversation history; it does not include passwords, authentication secrets, private storage paths or copies of the original uploaded files.

The Beta does not currently have a separate “delete this evidence” or “delete this conversation” control. Email us to request removal of specific information, withdraw from early access without deleting an account, or request deletion if you cannot sign in. We may need to verify that the request concerns your information.

Deletion from the live app does not automatically remove separate emails, provider logs, browser storage or historic backups. See section 10 for these limits.

15. Security

CareerKraft uses salted password hashing, protected server-side sessions, expiring single-use account links, authentication rate limits and access checks for private evidence and profile photos. API keys are kept on the server. The database, configuration and private files are not served as public website assets.

Production configuration requires an HTTPS application address and secure authentication cookies. Public hosting must also provide HTTPS correctly; a local development address is not a claim that transport is encrypted. File processing has type, size and resource limits, and uploaded code is not executed.

No service is completely secure. Use a unique password, keep verification and reset links private, and tell us promptly if you think an account or upload has been exposed.

16. Complaints and the ICO

You can raise a concern with CareerKraft at careerkraft.official@gmail.com. Explain what happened and how you would like us to help.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). Visit the ICO complaints page or its contact page. You do not have to give up this right by using CareerKraft.

For important changes to how personal information is used, we will bring them to users’ attention through a prominent notice or account email, as appropriate.
